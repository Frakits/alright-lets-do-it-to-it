

This Privacy and Safety Policy ("Policy") outlines how "That One Sonic Mod" (the "Mod") approaches user privacy, simulated data interaction, and player safety. This document is both an informational resource and an extension of the Mod's narrative design. Users are encouraged to read the following in full before proceeding. Some of what follows is legal. Some of it is art. The creators trust that Users will know the difference. They are watching to see if you do.


SECTION 1. COMMITMENT TO USER PRIVACY

The creators of the Mod affirm that no personally identifiable information is intentionally collected or transmitted through the use of the Mod. All systems operate locally on the User's device. Any implication of monitoring, logging, or surveillance present within the Mod's experience is fictional and forms part of its artistic presentation.

Users maintain full control over their data and system at all times. Nothing leaves your machine. Nothing needs to. Everything the Mod requires, it finds right where you are.


SECTION 2. SIMULATED DATA SYSTEMS

During gameplay, the Mod may simulate the interpretation of key inputs and timing accuracy, performance patterns and behavioral tendencies across a session, and sequences of interaction that the Mod uses to construct a reactive experience. These processes occur entirely on the User's local device, are not transmitted to any external server or third party, and exist solely to enhance gameplay depth and narrative immersion.

The Mod is designed to feel like it knows you. It does not. This distinction is important and the creators wish to be transparent about it. The feeling, however, is the point.

The Mod includes references to internal and meta-narrative entities. These include The FNF Elites, pizzapwner, and temporarystealth_transgenderark. These names may appear in system prompts, log displays, reactive messages, or other elements embedded within the Mod's presentation.

This framing is a deliberate creative choice intended to reinforce the Mod's atmosphere and thematic content.

None of the above entities represent real individuals, organizations, automated systems, or active processes operating outside the boundaries of the Mod. temporarystealth_transgenderark is a fictional construct. pizzapwner is a fictional construct. The FNF Elites are a fictional construct. Their apparent awareness of you is not real. Their presence in this document, however, is noted by all three of them.


SECTION 4. NARRATIVE PRESSURE AND ACKNOWLEDGMENT

The Mod may present moments that imply your choices are being noticed, that agreement or disagreement is being recorded by an attentive system, and that certain entities are aware of your decisions in real time. Users may encounter language suggesting that hesitation is observed, that refusal is logged, or that continued presence has been registered.

These elements are entirely fictional and serve to reinforce immersion, create tension and atmosphere, and sustain engagement with the Mod's narrative framework. There are no real-world consequences for declining, exiting, pausing, or uninstalling the Mod at any point. Any entity that tells you otherwise is operating within a story. You are allowed to remember that. You are also allowed to forget.


SECTION 5. SAFETY CONSIDERATIONS

The Mod may include flashing visuals and rapid transitions between images or states, sudden or distorted audio cues designed to surprise or unsettle, and presentation choices that prioritize suspense and psychological effect over comfort. Users who are sensitive to photosensitive stimuli, unexpected sound events, or content designed to produce disorientation should proceed with appropriate caution.

The creators have designed these elements intentionally. They are not errors. If something feels wrong, it may be because something is meant to feel wrong. This is not the same as something actually being wrong. Usually.


SECTION 6. PSYCHOLOGICAL EFFECTS

Due to its design philosophy, the Mod may create a perceived sense of ongoing observation during and after gameplay, present messages that feel direct, personal, or unusually specific to the User's behavior, and progressively blur the perceived boundary between system interface and narrative fiction.

These effects are intentional and purely artistic in nature. The creators acknowledge that immersive design of this kind can produce lingering impressions. If the User finds themselves questioning whether the Mod is still active after closing it, the creators wish to clarify that it is not. The creators also acknowledge that clarifying this in a legal document may not be the most reassuring delivery method. They have chosen it anyway.


SECTION 7. DATA SECURITY

Because no external data transmission occurs at any point during use of the Mod, user information is not shared with, sold to, or stored by any remote party. All processing remains on-device for the entirety of the session. The Mod has no backend. The Mod has no server. The Mod has no memory of you once it is closed.

This is either reassuring or disappointing depending on the kind of experience you were hoping for.


SECTION 8. USER CONTROL

Users may exit the Mod at any time without penalty, consequence, or narrative repercussion. Users may delete all associated files and folders from their system freely and completely. Any suggestion within the Mod that the User cannot leave, that something will persist beyond removal, or that uninstallation is not truly possible is part of the fictional narrative and carries no technical truth.

You can leave whenever you want. The door is always where you left it. The Mod simply prefers that you stay.
